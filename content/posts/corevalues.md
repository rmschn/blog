For many years I have shared the following Core Values
with teams and places I have worked. I figured why not share them here.

Now to be quite honest, I do not recall any longer if I built these on top
of something else, or if I just generated these from my own thoughts.
So any semblance to others is a happy accident of like mindedness.
Over the years, I have modified the wording a bit. I'd be curious to hear
about any additions or thoghts about these people may have.

The following is the current set:

**Core Values**

**People**

- Respect and care for well-being and professional development of yourself and
  others
- Maintain atmosphere comprised of trust, empowerment, teamwork
- Get the best people in the right place, including yourself

**Courage**

- Take risk when necessary
- Listen to our critics
- Respond to the challenge and opportunity presented as it presents

**Education**

- Recognize that life-long learning is a necessity
- Provide and/or seek opportunity for further learning

**Empowerment**

- Provide tools and information needed to create opportunity
- Enable the decision maker, the innovator, the doer

**Teamwork**

- Work together with common purpose, goals, creating a culture and atmosphere
  where everyone has a chance to contribute and achieve success as we strive
  towards delivering on our mission

**Integrity**

- Say what you believe, do what you say! Be WYSIWYG!
- Work with trust, honesty, caring, fairness, loyalty and diversity

**Attitude**

- Reflects these values
- Be enthusiastic, proactive and passionate

**Accountability**

- By being accountable, you earn the right to hold others accountable.
  Execute driving success. Deliver!

**Quality**

- Expect nothing but the best from you, your team and others around you
- All of the above are necessary to achieve quality.
