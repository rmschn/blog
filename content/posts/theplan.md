# Brief History

I've never managed to be very consistent at doing this. I think it part its because
I do not want to contribute to the mess that is online these days. That said, I find
it incredibly fun to learn from others on the internet. So I keep revisiting this
idea of writing regularly on my own site for the benefit of others. This is my
latest attempt beginning here in 2026. I have a plan...

# New Plan

So whats the new plan?

Well, consistency and scheduling. This site is going to be a place where I share
ideas about technologies from a variety of view points, but mostly my own. These
viewpoints will be informed by my own experiences and will likely come with some
exposure to my sense of humor. :) 

I'm going to try to keep it valuable to others. I'm going to try to keep it focused
to technology and areas in technology that I have many years of experience and of
course newer technologies where I am simply continually interested.

I am a life long learner and so I hope everyone that reads anything on this site
gains from that time spent. 

# Tentative Schedule

So whats the schedule?

My current plan is to post weekly. Likely at the end of each week on a Friday. For
now I'm going to leave it at that. I'm not going to set a time. Maybe later if this
gets more organized. 

So, if interested, plan on visiting on Fridays to check out whats been posted. 

# Thanks for Interest

Thanks for being here. Thanks for being interested. Hope to see you here next Friday
for the first technical post!
